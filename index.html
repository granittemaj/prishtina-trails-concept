    How do I register?

    Registration is fully online through our ticketing page. Spaces are limited, so early registration is recommended.

    Which distance should I choose?

    The 10K Base is perfect for beginners. The 23K Half suits experienced trail runners, and the 50K Ultra is for highly skilled trail runners. There are dedicated kids’ races too.

    What gear is mandatory?

    10K: bib and 500 ml water. 23K: bib, 500 ml water, food (gels) and a first aid kit. 50K: bib, 1 L water, food (gels), first aid kit, mobile phone, drinking cup and emergency blanket.

    Does my result count for UTMB or ITRA?

    Yes. Prishtina Trails is recognised by both the UTMB Index and ITRA.

    Will there be cups at the aid stations?

    No. We provide the water; you bring your own bottle or reusable cup. There is no single-use plastic during the race.

    Can I donate to charity?

    Yes. When you register you can add a donation of any amount to a Kosovo organisation of your choice. We pass every donation on.

    Where can I read the full rules?

    Everything from eligibility and cut-offs to environmental rules and penalties.
